# Orphanage NGO Website — Page-by-Page Content Outline

## 1. Home
- **Hero section**: Mission tagline + strong photo/video + primary CTAs ("Donate Now" / "Volunteer With Us")
- **Impact snapshot**: Key stats (children supported, years active, programs run, meals served, etc.)
- **Mission statement**: 2–3 sentence summary of what the NGO does and why
- **Featured story**: One recent blog/story teaser with "Read More" link
- **Programs overview**: Short cards linking to Programs page (education, healthcare, shelter, etc.)
- **Get Involved teaser**: Volunteer + donate + sponsor-a-child options, each with a short blurb
- **Testimonials/quotes**: From volunteers, donors, or partner organizations
- **Newsletter signup**: Email capture
- **Trust signals**: Registration/certification logos, partner logos, financial transparency badge

## 2. About Us
- **Our story**: Founding history, why the NGO started
- **Mission & vision**: Clear statements
- **Values**: 3–5 core principles guiding the work
- **Leadership & team**: Bios of founders/directors/key staff (with photos)
- **Board of directors** (if applicable): Names + short bios, for credibility
- **Registration & legitimacy**: Legal status, registration number, certifications, audited financials link
- **Timeline/milestones**: Optional visual history of growth

## 3. Our Programs
- **Overview**: What programs exist (education, healthcare, nutrition, shelter, aftercare, etc.)
- **Individual program sections**: Each with goal, activities, and impact so far
- **How funds are used per program**: Brief transparency note
- *(Note: avoid identifying individual children by name/photo without documented consent + safeguarding review)*

## 4. Stories / Blog (staff-managed content hub)
- **Category structure**: e.g., Success Stories, Events, Announcements, Volunteer Spotlights
- **Post template fields**: Title, featured image, date, category tag, body content, optional author byline
- **Archive/filter view**: By category or date
- **Social share buttons** on each post
- *(This is the main area non-technical staff will use regularly — should have the simplest possible editor)*

## 5. Get Involved
- **Volunteer opportunities**: Roles available, time commitment, requirements
- **Volunteer application form**: Name, contact, skills, availability, background check note
- **Corporate/group volunteering**: If applicable
- **In-kind donations**: Wishlist of needed items (supplies, books, clothing)
- **Internship opportunities**: If relevant

## 6. Donate
- **Donation options**: One-time, monthly recurring, sponsor-a-child (if used)
- **Impact per amount**: e.g., "$25 provides school supplies for a month"
- **Payment integration**: Stripe/PayPal/local payment gateway
- **Transparency section**: Link to annual report/financials
- **Tax receipt info**: If applicable in their country
- **Other ways to give**: Bank transfer, planned giving, corporate sponsorship

## 7. Gallery / Media
- **Photo galleries**: Organized by event/program (with privacy safeguards for children)
- **Videos**: Embedded YouTube/Vimeo content
- **Press/media mentions**: News coverage, press kit download

## 8. Contact
- **Contact form**: General inquiries
- **Physical address & map embed**
- **Phone/email**
- **Social media links**
- **FAQ section** (optional): Common donor/volunteer questions

## 9. (Optional) Annual Reports / Transparency Page
- Downloadable PDFs of annual reports, financial statements
- Builds major trust with larger donors/grant-makers

---

## Content ownership notes for staff CMS access
- **Blog/Stories**: Full add/edit access — this is their main regular posting area
- **Home featured story**: Auto-pull latest blog post, or manually pin one
- **Programs/About**: Editable but changed infrequently — consider limiting edit access to a lead admin
- **Donate/payment settings**: Locked down, admin-only
